# Deleting your DIAL Nutrition data

**App:** DIAL Nutrition
**Developer:** Rohail Naqvi
**Contact:** dial.nutrition.app@gmail.com

DIAL Nutrition stores your data on your own device. There is no account, no server, and no copy held by the developer — so deleting your data does not require a request to anyone. You can do it yourself, at any time, and it is immediate.

## How to delete your data

### Delete individual entries
Open the app and remove any meal, water, weight, or recipe entry directly. Deleted entries are removed from your records.

### Delete your conversation history with Addie
Open Addie, tap the **⋯** menu in the header, and choose **Clear chat history**. Conversations are also deleted automatically after three days.

### Delete everything
Uninstall DIAL Nutrition from your device. This permanently destroys the app's encrypted database and the encryption key held in your device's secure storage. Once uninstalled, the data cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

## What is deleted

Uninstalling the app removes all of the following from your device:

- Your profile (name, date of birth, gender, height, activity level, units)
- Goals, calorie and macro targets, and their history
- All logged meals, water, weight and vitals entries
- Saved recipes and any recipe cover images you added
- Your conversation history with Addie
- App settings, including your Google Gemini API key
- Cached activity data read from Health Connect

## What is not deleted, and why

**Backup files you created.** If you exported an encrypted backup (a `.dialb` file) to Google Drive, your device's storage, or anywhere else, that file is yours and stays where you put it. Delete it yourself if you no longer want it. Nobody else can open it without your passphrase.

**Data you sent to Google via Addie.** If you used the optional AI assistant, the messages you sent were transmitted to Google under **your own Google Gemini API key**. That data is held by Google under your agreement with them, not by the developer of DIAL Nutrition. To manage or delete it, see Google's privacy tools for your Google account and the [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

**Health Connect data.** DIAL Nutrition only reads from Health Connect; it never writes to it. Uninstalling DIAL removes its access. Data written by your health or fitness app remains in Health Connect and is managed there.

## Retention

There is no retention period, because there is no server. Data exists on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
