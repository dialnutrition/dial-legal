## 2. AI-generated content can be wrong

Addie is an AI assistant. **AI makes mistakes.** Macro and calorie estimates (especially from photos), target suggestions, recipes, and coaching commentary may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate for your situation — even when they sound confident. Photo-based estimates are approximations by nature.

You are responsible for reviewing AI output before relying on it. Verify anything that matters — especially where allergies, medical conditions, or significant dietary changes are involved. Addie's allergy awareness is a convenience feature, **not a safety system**: always verify ingredients yourself.

The App also makes one small judgement of its own: it checks whether your goal type, current weight and target weight contradict each other, and tells Addie when they do, so that she does not coach you toward a goal your own settings rule out. That check is a simple comparison of the numbers you entered, not an assessment of whether a goal is healthy or appropriate for you. It is described in the Privacy Policy, section 4.
